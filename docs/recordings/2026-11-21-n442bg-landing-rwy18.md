What it flew: northbound about 1.9 km east of runway 18/36 at pattern altitude (left downwind, extended well past the threshold), a left turn westbound about 2.8 km north of the threshold (base), then southbound on the centerline (final), down to about 90 ft above the field 570 m from the threshold when adsb.lol lost it.

What the app showed at the time, with the runway setting at its default of 28: "Entering the pattern · RWY 28", "Departing · RWY 28" and mostly nothing. Wrong, because the runway in use was 18.

Replayed through `classify()` with runway 18: downwind (0.94–0.99), "entry" for two checks during the downwind-to-base turn (wrong: it was turning base), base (0.72–0.95), final (0.92–1.00).

What this showed needed fixing (all three done the same night; this recording is replayed in `app/android/scripts/test-list.mjs`):

1. **The runway in use has to be detected**, not just set by hand. A plane on final says which runway it is.
2. **The 45° entry rule fires during a wide base turn.** It should only apply abeam the runway.
3. **Stale positions.** After adsb.lol lost the plane the app kept moving it from the same last report, and ran its altitude down to 0 ft. The age of each position (`seen_pos`) has to be counted.
