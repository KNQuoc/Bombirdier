Radio capture

Sends the sound of a radio feed playing in another Chrome tab to Bombirdier on this Mac, which writes down each call and shows it on the phone.

  1. Open the feed's page and press the green LISTEN next to the feed. Its player opens in a new tab named "Listening to: …" and starts playing. Leave that tab open.
  2. Come back here and press Capture tab for that feed.
  3. In Chrome's window, choose the Chrome Tab named "Listening to: …" (the player, not the page with the LISTEN button), leave Also share tab audio on, then Share.
