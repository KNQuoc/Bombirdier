⚙ Settings

The view shows live planes within the AR radius of your GPS position; the ☰ list covers everything within 250 nm. Planes in the wrong direction? Calibrate the compass.
Voice: tilt your head to one side and hold it for a second (phone in hand: tap with two fingers), wait for the beep, then ask ("what's that plane?", "show me the helicopters", "next page"). Needs the Mac server running (mac-server/run.sh) and the phone plugged into the Mac, or on Wi-Fi with a server token.
In view: ☰ top right = plane list (tap a row to open it, ▲▼ or swipe to page) · double-tap middle = fine-tune (then middle = next setting, left/right = adjust) · ⚙ or long-press = these settings · back = exit

🧭 Compass calibration

Hold the phone and slowly draw a big figure 8 in the air, twisting your wrist so the screen faces up, down and to each side. Keep away from metal, magnets and electronics.
